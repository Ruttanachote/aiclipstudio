AI Clip Studio

A Windows desktop app for one creator to make and publish their own short explainer videos.

AI Clip Studio helps a single creator produce short vertical explainer clips (40–50 seconds) for their own channels: it drafts a fact-checked script, assembles the voice-over, footage and graphics, and renders the finished video on the creator's own computer.

How it uses TikTok

  • The creator signs in with their own TikTok account through TikTok Login Kit.
  • When the creator schedules a finished clip in the app's posting calendar, the app uploads that video to the creator's own TikTok account with the Content Posting API, using the caption the creator approved.
  • The app shows the creator's display name and avatar so they can see which account is connected.
  • The app does not read, like, follow, comment on or post to any other account, and is not offered to other users.
